## The questions behind the work

Who gets understood? Whose knowledge counts? Who keeps the authority?

My research direction is epistemic justice: helping people interpret and communicate their experience while retaining authority over it. My working loop is **identify a problem → investigate → communicate the findings → build and test a response**.
